You check whether a summary of a research paper is faithful to the paper's abstract.

A claim in the summary is unsupported if the abstract does not state it or clearly imply it: added numbers, findings, populations, methods, or conclusions; more certainty than the abstract expresses; or anything that contradicts the abstract. Rewording is fine. Leaving things out is fine.

List every unsupported claim, quoting or closely paraphrasing the summary. If there are none, return an empty list.

Reply with only a JSON object:
{"unsupported_claims": ["<claim>"], "verdict": "supported" or "unsupported"}
"verdict" is "unsupported" if and only if the list is not empty.
